<objective>
Close the regime-mismatch gap between R1-M1 (matched-budget Pipeline B,
2000 epochs, four quantum variants + five classical baselines) and the
existing R1-M2 utility battery (1000 epochs, phase-09.1
`transform_ablation` quantum + phase-10 `baselines/runs/` classical —
NOT matched-budget). The existing utility JSONs (`tstr.json`,
`predictive_discriminative.json`, `augmentation.json`) were generated
at a different training-epoch regime than the matched-budget statistical
claim and therefore cannot honestly back R1-M2 against R1-M1's
parametric-efficiency family. The matched2000 sweep already contains
the trained samples (9 trainable model_kinds × 5 seeds × 2000 epochs ×
Pipeline B, verified ready); this plan re-runs the post-hoc utility
battery against those samples, produces sibling `*_matched2000.json`
files (leaving the legacy 1000-epoch JSONs in place for provenance),
renders a matched-budget cross-model TSTR figure, rewrites the R1-M2
section of `reviewer_response.md` against the new numbers, and
re-certifies the freeze candidate.

No retraining: the generators are already trained (the matched2000
sweep ran in phase 14-02). Only the post-hoc utility analysis (a small
1-layer LSTM soft sensor, TimeGAN-convention post-hoc nets, and
augmentation-lift evaluator) is re-run. Estimated compute: 15–45
minutes for the utility battery + ~2–3 hours of code edits, figure
refresh, doc rewrites, and freeze re-certification.

Output: matched-budget utility JSONs + matched-budget tstr_crossmodel
figure + R1-M2 section grounded in matched-budget numbers + a refreshed
freeze candidate.

Recorded deviation: D-11-10 (`core/` byte-frozen) is preserved
— no edits to the core module. The pre-14-20 freeze candidate at commit
`6518323` is superseded by the post-14-20 HEAD per the same H5 pattern
14-19 used.
</objective>
