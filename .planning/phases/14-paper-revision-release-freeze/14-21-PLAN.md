# Plan 14-21 — Fix x0.1 WGAN inverse-pipeline bug; re-run matched-budget metrics + figures + paper updates

## Goal

Repair the structural x0.1 attenuation bug in the WGAN inverse pipeline that systematically under-reports quantum and classical-WGAN sample distributions by a factor of 10 in the matched-budget evaluation/figure code. Apply the inference-only correction (multiply by 10 inside every samples.npy-consuming producer, gated on `model_kind in _WGAN_KINDS`), re-run all matched-budget metric JSONs + the frozen-checkpoint headline JSON, regenerate ~200 figure triples, refresh every paper literal that derives from a regenerated JSON, re-assess the bifurcated finding (LR-DTW dominance + OD-EMD parametric equivalence) against the corrected numbers, update the abstract + contributions list accordingly, and disclose the bug + fix in the supplement methodology. **Source of truth: `/Users/shawngibford/.claude/plans/cached-tumbling-starfish.md`** (user-approved 7-step plan from 2026-06-10). This PLAN.md is a faithful structured restatement of that artifact, surgically corrected against the orchestrator's grep ground-truth (the original draft mis-identified two of the six target-JSON producers).

## Context (from prior work)

- **Smoking gun**: `archive/qgan_pennylane_SEL.py:661-663` comment "Real data is roughly in [-0.08, 0.09], so scale by ~0.1" — the x0.1 was designed for Pipeline A (raw log-returns at [-0.08, +0.09]). Pipeline B switched real-sample range to [-1, +1] but the x0.1 was preserved verbatim. The inverse formula `((s+1)/2)*(r_max-r_min)+r_min` assumes `s ∈ [-1, +1]` but `samples.npy` for all WGAN-trained models actually contains `s ∈ [-0.1, +0.1]`.
- **Empirically confirmed in this session**: IQP:SEL gen log-delta std AS-IS = 0.085× real → with x10 fix = 0.848× real; V1/V2/V3 same pattern (0.07-0.12× → 0.73-1.16×). Published IQP:SEL seed-42 LR-EMD = 0.0149 reproduces exactly from the buggy x0.1 sample space; with fix → 0.0034 (4× lower).
- **Bug consumers identified (grep-verified by the orchestrator just now)**: NINE `samples_pm1 = np.load(...)` sites across SEVEN producer files. Each producer file is paper-cited (verified by grep against `main (4) copy.tex`, `paper/supp_material.tex`, and the three paper-blocks docs):
  1. `run_matched2000_dualscale.py:189` → emits `matched2000_dualscale.json` (extensively cited in reviewer_response.md + main.tex)
  2. `run_distribution_emd.py:217` → emits `distribution_emd.json` (cited in reviewer_response.md:474)
  3. `run_utility.py:170` → emits `tstr_matched2000.json` + `augmentation_matched2000.json` (cited in reviewer_response.md:37)
  4. `run_timegan_scores.py:165` → emits `predictive_discriminative_matched2000.json` (cited in reviewer_response.md:37)
  5. `run_ansatz_comparison.py:159` → emits `ansatz_comparison.json` (cited in reviewer_response.md:65 + paper_blocks_framing.md:399/465/518) — ansatz_comparison operates on the 4 quantum variants which all sit inside `_WGAN_KINDS` (every row gets x10)
  6. `run_canonical_headline.py:210` + `:232` → emits `headline_canonical.json` (load-bearing for the entire revision). This file BOTH applies x0.1 at sample generation AND runs the Pipeline-B inverse — it must be patched at one or the other site so the net effect is x10.
  7. `run_figure_suite.py:272` (inside `reconstruct_od`), `:843` (inside `render_reconstruction_overlay` — the 3-panel time-series overlay producer), and `:3118` (inside `main()`, the sweep entry-point for cross-model figure generation). The OTHER per-model renderers (`render_distribution_comparison`, `render_qq_plot`, `render_stylized_facts`, `render_logreturn_stat_grid`, `render_dtw_alignment`) take pre-computed `od` / `gen_log_delta` arrays as parameters — they do NOT load samples.npy themselves and need NO direct patching.
- **VAE/AR are excluded** per Pitfall 3 (`run_baselines.py:28`) — they were trained WITHOUT the x0.1 and their `samples.npy` is already in `[-1, +1]` space. Applying x10 to them would break their published metrics.
- **`run_dualscale_fidelity.py:206` is the OLD 1000ep / Phase-11 driver** (emits `fidelity_dualscale.json`). It is referenced in `reviewer_response.md` (R1-M3) but the 14-20 reviewer-response now anchors the matched-budget headline to `matched2000_dualscale.json` (Plan 14-20). The 1000ep driver is patched for completeness/transparency but is not on the matched-budget hot-path.
- **welch_pairwise.json** is a pure aggregator over `matched2000_dualscale.json` (per `run_welch_aggregator.py:34`). It is regenerated in T02 by re-running the aggregator after `matched2000_dualscale.json` is itself refreshed.
- **Inference-only fix authorized**: user explicitly chose x10-at-inverse over a multi-day retraining effort. Training-time x0.1 sites (`run_matched2000.py:281`, `core/training.py:347,381,416`, `run_baselines.py:205`) MUST NOT be touched — they define the `samples.npy` on-disk contract.
- **High-risk re-assessment at Step 5**: the paper's bifurcated finding may shift qualitatively after the fix. User accepted this risk but required a pause-and-confirm gate before any narrative rewrite.
